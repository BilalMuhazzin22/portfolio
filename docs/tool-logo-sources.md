## Monochrome workflow marks

Simple Icons brand vectors from https://api.iconify.design/simple-icons/ are used in white for Google Ads, Meta, Google Analytics, Search Console, Semrush, Business Profile, Canva, OpenAI, WordPress and Blogger. Ubersuggest and Adobe Express use the existing sourced product artwork with SVG luminance filters to render the marks in white.
